Privacy Policy

BKVerse Publisher · Last updated: 21 November 2026

BKVerse Publisher is an internal tool operated by BKVerse (bkverse.com). It is used only by the BKVerse team to upload videos that we produce to social media accounts that we own or manage. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

  • When an account owner connects TikTok, Instagram or X, we receive the basic account identifier and an access token granted by that platform.
  • We access only the permissions needed to upload or publish our own content (for example TikTok video.upload, Instagram content publishing, X post writing).

How we store it

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted in the operating system keychain of the BKVerse computer that runs the tool. They are not stored on any server or shared with anyone.
  • Videos are sent directly from our computer to the platform. We keep no copies of other users' content.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • We do not access followers, messages or other people's personal data.

Deleting data

An account owner can disconnect at any time from the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). On request, we delete the stored token immediately. Contact: batu@bkverse.com.
